WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
Important accuracy note
A single image has no built-in real-world ruler. The app reports visible 2D spans in pixels. Hidden depth and true 3D length/breadth/height cannot be reliably calculated without scale/depth information. Region detection is a pure visual heuristic, not a universal object-recognition model.
Private by design · no external services, paid APIs, neural-network downloads or third-party libraries. The backend analyses a reduced RGB representation of the image. Results depend on contrast and scene complexity.
